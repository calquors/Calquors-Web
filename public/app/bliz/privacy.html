Who we are

Bliz is an Android social app for live voice rooms, one-to-one voice and video calls, and chat. It is operated by CALQUORS PRIVATE LIMITED ("Calquors", "we"), Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, India. This policy explains what personal data Bliz collects, why, which app permissions it uses, who data is shared with and the choices you have. Contact support@calquors.com with any privacy question or request.

Information we collect

  • Mobile number and name you provide at sign-up, verified as described in the next section.
  • Google account (optional): if you link Google, we receive your Google account ID, email address and name so you can sign in with Google later. Google cannot be used to create a new account.
  • Profile: display name, gender, age or date of birth, languages, interests, bio, and a profile photo (chosen with Android's photo picker or taken with the camera) or an in-app cartoon avatar.
  • Host application: if you apply to become a host, the selfie and short voice introduction you record, used to review your application and confirm you are a real person.
  • Activity and content: chat messages and media you send, rooms you create or join, follows, blocks, reports, gifts, game moves inside rooms, referral codes and daily rewards.
  • Coins, diamonds and purchases: coin balance, Google Play order IDs, call minutes billed, diamonds earned by hosts and withdrawal requests. We never receive your full card, UPI or bank login details from Google Play.
  • Device and technical data: device model, OS version, app version, push notification token, IP address, connection and error logs, used to run the service, prevent fraud and fix problems.

Signing in and phone number verification

Every account is tied to a verified mobile number. Depending on your device and the options available in the app, Bliz verifies it in one of these ways:

  • Truecaller one-tap (if the Truecaller app is installed): with your consent on Truecaller's screen, Truecaller shares your verified mobile number with Bliz. No extra permission is needed.
  • Missed-call verification (for people without Truecaller): Truecaller places a short missed call to the number you enter. The Truecaller SDK inside Bliz uses the phone state, call log and answer-calls permissions to detect that one incoming call, confirm it came from Truecaller and end it automatically. Bliz does not read, upload or store your call history or contacts, and these permissions are requested only on the mobile number screen.
  • SMS code: we send a one-time code to your number through our SMS provider. Android's SMS consent prompt lets you share only that one message with Bliz; Bliz does not read your other SMS and does not request the SMS permission.

In every method our server re-checks the result before creating your session. Truecaller processes data for verification under its own privacy policy. We do not use verification data for advertising and do not sell it.

Calls, video and face checks

One-to-one calls connect directly between devices (WebRTC) where possible, or through an encrypted relay. Voice rooms are routed through Cloudflare's real-time media servers. We do not record or store the audio or video of calls or voice rooms. Other participants may still record what they hear or see, so do not share sensitive information.

During video calls Bliz runs an automatic face check to confirm that a real person's face is in view, which helps prevent fake, blank or abusive video. The check is not used to identify who you are, and we do not create or keep facial recognition templates from it.

How we use information

  • Create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • Provide voice rooms, voice and video calls, chat, matchmaking, games, gifts, rewards and referrals.
  • Review host applications and keep hosts genuine.
  • Process coin purchases, per-minute call billing, host diamonds and withdrawals.
  • Send notifications you allow, such as messages, call alerts and room invites.
  • Prevent fraud, multiple accounts and abuse; enforce our Terms and Community Guidelines; respond to reports.
  • Diagnose errors and improve reliability. We do not sell personal data and do not show ads.

Who we share it with

Other users see your public profile, what you say or show in rooms and calls, and messages you send them. Service providers process data only to run Bliz: Truecaller (phone verification), our SMS provider (delivering login codes), Supabase (database, storage and real-time messaging), Railway (application server hosting), Cloudflare (voice room media and call relay), and Google (Google Play Billing, Google Sign-In and Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications). If host withdrawals are offered, identity verification (KYC) and payouts are handled by regulated payment and KYC partners, who receive only the details needed for that purpose. We may disclose information where required by law, by a lawful order of an Indian authority, or to protect users and the service. Some providers may process data outside India.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. A host selfie and voice introduction are kept while you are a host or your application is under review. You can delete your account in the app or by email; see Delete account. Deletion removes your profile, photos, host selfie and voice intro, messages and linked data managed by the service. Limited records (purchase and payout transactions, fraud-prevention markers such as whether a phone number already received a welcome bonus, moderation and legal records) may be kept as long as needed for those purposes or as required by law. Copies saved by other users are outside our control.

Your rights and choices

You can edit your profile, unlink Google, block or report users and manage permissions in Android settings. Under applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may request access to, correction or erasure of your personal data, withdraw consent, and nominate another person to exercise your rights. Email support@calquors.com; we may need to verify that you own the account.

Grievance officer

Grievance Officer, Calquors Private Limited, Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, India. Email: support@calquors.com (subject: Bliz grievance). We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within 15 days.

Adults only, security and changes

Bliz is only for people aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if you believe a minor is using Bliz, report the account or email us and we will remove it. We use encryption in transit, access controls and row-level database security, but no system is completely secure. If we change this policy, we will update the date above and give additional notice in the app where required.
