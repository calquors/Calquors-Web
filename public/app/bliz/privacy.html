Who we are

Bliz is an Android social app for live voice rooms, one-to-one voice and video calls, and chat. It is operated by CALQUORS PRIVATE LIMITED ("Calquors", "we"), Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, India. This policy explains what personal data Bliz collects, why, which app permissions it uses, who data is shared with and the choices you have. Contact support@calquors.com with any privacy question or request.

Information we collect

  • Mobile number and name you provide at sign-up, verified as described in the next section.
  • Google account (optional): if you link Google, we receive your Google account ID, email address and name so you can sign in with Google later. Google cannot be used to create a new account.
  • Profile: display name, gender, age or date of birth, languages, interests, bio, and a profile photo (chosen with Android's photo picker or taken with the camera) or an in-app cartoon avatar.
  • Host application: your legal name (as on your ID), date of birth, address and city, a selfie, a short voice introduction and the call rates you choose, used to review your application and confirm you are a real person. Your date of birth and address are encrypted before they are stored.
  • Payout verification (hosts who withdraw earnings): legal name, PAN, date of birth, a photo of your PAN card, a fresh selfie, and the UPI ID or bank account and IFSC where you want to be paid (in your own name). We ask for this only with your consent, shown on the payout details screen. We check these ourselves; a reviewer compares the PAN photo with your selfie and confirms the PAN with the Income Tax Department. We do not collect Aadhaar numbers or Aadhaar card images.
  • Activity and content: chat messages (text) you send, including messages to AI friends, rooms you create or join, follows, blocks, reports, gifts, game moves inside rooms, referral codes and daily rewards.
  • Coins, diamonds and purchases: coin balance, Google Play order IDs, call minutes billed, diamonds earned by hosts and withdrawal requests. We never receive your full card, UPI or bank login details from Google Play.
  • Device and technical data: device model, OS version, app version, push notification token, IP address, connection and error logs, used to run the service, prevent fraud and fix problems.

Signing in and phone number verification

Every account is tied to a verified mobile number. Bliz verifies it in one of these ways:

  • Truecaller one-tap (if the Truecaller app is installed): with your consent on Truecaller's screen, Truecaller shares your verified mobile number with Bliz. No extra permission is needed.
  • SMS code: if you don't have the Truecaller app, we send a one-time code by SMS through our SMS provider. Android shows a one-time prompt to share only that message with Bliz; Bliz does not request the SMS permission and does not read your other messages.

In both methods our server re-checks the result before creating your session. Truecaller processes data for verification under its own privacy policy. We do not use verification data for advertising and do not sell it.

Calls, video and face checks

One-to-one calls connect directly between devices (WebRTC) where possible, or through an encrypted relay. Voice rooms are routed through Cloudflare's real-time media servers. We do not record or store the audio or video of calls or voice rooms. Other participants may still record what they hear or see, so do not share sensitive information.

During video calls Bliz runs an automatic face check to confirm that a real person's face is in view, which helps prevent fake, blank or abusive video. The check is not used to identify who you are, and we do not create or keep facial recognition templates from it.

AI friends

Bliz has AI chat companions (currently Bliz, Elven and Mark). They are always marked with an "AI" badge, and a note at the top of their chats says replies are generated by AI. They are not real people.

When you message an AI friend, the recent messages in that chat (up to the last 24) are sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, PBC (United States), to write the reply. We do not send your phone number or other profile details with them, but anything you type in that chat is sent. Please don't share personal details with AI friends.

AI friends only chat. They do not give advice, and their replies can be wrong. You can send up to 50 messages a day to AI friends, free. You can report or block an AI friend like any other profile. AI chats are deleted with your account, like your other messages.

Payout verification

Payout verification details are used only to confirm the host's identity, pay their earnings into their own account, prevent fraud (one PAN can be linked to only one Bliz account) and meet tax law, including deducting and reporting tax where required. They are not used for anything else.

Our team checks the PAN with the Income Tax Department's online verification service, compares the PAN card photo with the host's selfie, and confirms with the bank or UPI system that the payout account is registered in the host's name. Hosts are never asked to pay anything for this check. Before a selfie is accepted, the app checks on the phone that one face is clearly visible; this check does not identify anyone and nothing from it is stored.

How they are protected: PAN, date of birth, UPI ID and bank account number are encrypted before they are stored, and the app only ever shows them masked (for example XXXXX1234X). Only authorised Calquors staff can view them, and every view or export is logged. The PAN card photo and the selfie are deleted as soon as they have been reviewed.

Your choices: a host can see the masked details on file, correct them by submitting them again, or remove them at any time from Host studio > Payout details. Without approved payout details a host cannot withdraw.

How we use information

  • Create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • Provide voice rooms, voice and video calls, chat, AI friends, matchmaking, games, gifts, rewards and referrals.
  • Review host applications and keep hosts genuine.
  • Process coin purchases, per-minute call billing, host diamonds and withdrawals.
  • Send notifications you allow, such as messages, call alerts and room invites.
  • Prevent fraud, multiple accounts and abuse; enforce our Terms and Community Guidelines; respond to reports.
  • Diagnose errors and improve reliability. We do not sell personal data and do not show ads.

Who we share it with

Other users see your public profile, what you say or show in rooms and calls, and messages you send them. Service providers process data only to run Bliz: Truecaller (phone verification), our SMS provider (delivering login codes), Supabase (database, storage and real-time messaging), Railway (application server hosting), Cloudflare (voice room media and call relay), Google (Google Play Billing, Google Sign-In and Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications), and Anthropic (AI replies for AI friends; United States). Payouts: we verify payout details ourselves (see Payout verification) and pay approved withdrawals by bank transfer or UPI through our bank, which receives the host's name and UPI ID or bank account for that payment. Tax details of payouts (name, PAN and amounts) are shared with our chartered accountant and with the Income Tax Department as tax law requires. We may disclose information where required by law, by a lawful order of an Indian authority, or to protect users and the service. Some providers, including Anthropic, process data outside India.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. A host selfie and voice introduction are kept while you are a host or your application is under review.

Payout verification: your PAN card photo and the verification selfie are deleted as soon as the review is done, whether it is approved or rejected. PAN, date of birth and payout details are encrypted before storage and kept until you remove them or delete your account; details that were rejected and not corrected are deleted after 30 days. The copy of your payout details used for a withdrawal is wiped once it is paid or rejected. Records the law requires us to keep for tax (name, PAN, amount paid and bank reference) are kept, encrypted, for 8 years, even after the account is deleted, then deleted automatically. Every time our staff view or export this data, it is logged.

Deleting your account: you can delete your account in the app or by email; see Delete account. Deletion removes your profile, photos, host application details, selfie and voice intro, messages (including chats with AI friends), payout details and linked data managed by the service. Limited records (purchase and payout transactions, fraud-prevention markers such as whether a phone number already received a welcome bonus, moderation and legal records) may be kept as long as needed for those purposes or as required by law. Copies saved by other users are outside our control.

Your rights and choices

You can edit your profile, unlink Google, block or report users and manage permissions in Android settings. Hosts can view, correct or remove their payout details in Host studio. Under applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may request access to, correction or erasure of your personal data, withdraw consent, and nominate another person to exercise your rights. Email support@calquors.com; we may need to verify that you own the account.
