About

Bliz is a voice-first social app. Drop into a live voice room, talk one-to-one with someone new on a voice or video call, play games like Antakshari and Truth or Dare with a room, and keep chatting with the people you click with.

Features

Live voice rooms

Join a room, raise your hand and speak, or just listen.

Voice and video calls

Private one-to-one calls with hosts and friends. Your first call is free.

Private chat

Keep the conversation going after a room or call.

Room games

Truth or Dare, Antakshari and Chor Police with everyone in the room.

Daily rewards

Check in daily, keep a streak and invite friends to earn coins.

Verified people

Every account has a verified mobile number, and hosts are checked with a selfie and voice intro.

Safety

Bliz is for adults aged 18 and older. Follow our Community Guidelines, use report and block when needed, and never share money, UPI or bank details with people you meet in the app.
