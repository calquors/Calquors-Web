Our commitment

Bliz, operated by CALQUORS PRIVATE LIMITED ("Calquors", "we"), has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Any content, conversation or behaviour that sexualises, grooms, exploits or endangers a child is strictly prohibited on Bliz: in voice rooms, one-to-one voice and video calls, chats, profiles, photos and posts.

Bliz is for adults only

  • Bliz is only for people aged 18 and older. Everyone setting up an account must give an age of 18 or older and confirm that they are 18 or older before they can continue.
  • Before using Bliz, every user accepts our Terms, Community Guidelines and a safety pledge that misuse involving minors can lead to a permanent ban and a report to the police and other authorities under Indian law.
  • Accounts that appear to belong to someone under 18 are removed.

What is prohibited

  • Creating, sharing, requesting or linking to child sexual abuse material of any kind, including drawn, edited or AI-generated material.
  • Grooming: building a relationship with a minor, or with someone presenting as a minor, for sexual purposes.
  • Sexualised conversation, images, calls or roleplay involving minors.
  • Sextortion, trafficking, or any attempt to meet, contact or move a minor to another app or platform.

How to report

  • In the app: use Report on any profile, chat, voice room or call and choose "Seems under 18" or "Nudity or sexual content". You can block the person at the same time.
  • By email: write to support@calquors.com with the subject Bliz child safety. Include the person's Bliz name or profile link and what happened.

Our safety team reviews reports within 24 hours and treats child safety reports as the highest priority.

What we do

  • Remove the content and permanently ban the accounts involved, and take steps to stop them from returning.
  • Preserve the relevant information and report it to the appropriate authorities, including Indian law enforcement through the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in), as required by Indian law, including the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.
  • Cooperate with law enforcement requests made through proper legal channels.

Compliance

Bliz complies with applicable child safety laws and with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.

Child safety contact

Our designated point of contact for child safety and CSAM prevention can be reached at support@calquors.com.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services right away. In India, call 112, or the Childline helpline at 1098.
