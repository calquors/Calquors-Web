Summary

Bliz sells only digital items (coins) inside the app. All coin purchases are made through Google Play, so Google Play processes the payment and any refund. Bliz has no subscriptions and nothing is shipped.

Prices and packs may change; the price shown at checkout applies.

Refunds for coin purchases

  • Payment taken but coins not received: email us with your Google Play order ID (starts with GPA.). We will either credit the coins or arrange a refund.
  • Charged twice or accidental purchase: request a refund from Google Play (Play Store > Payments & subscriptions > Budget & history), or email us with the order ID. Unused coins from an accidental purchase reported within 7 days are eligible for a refund.
  • Coins already used for calls, gifts or other features are not refundable, except where Google Play policy or applicable law requires.
  • Free coins (welcome bonus, daily rewards, streaks and referral rewards) have no cash value and are never refunded.
  • Approved refunds are paid by Google Play to your original payment method. The time to reach your account depends on Google Play and your bank or UPI provider. The refunded coins are removed from your balance.
  • If a purchase is refunded or charged back (through Google Play or your bank), the coins are removed from your wallet. Coins you already spent become an amount you owe, shown in your wallet; your next purchase pays it back first. Two refunds or chargebacks within 90 days suspend the account automatically while we review it. Email support@calquors.com (subject "Bliz refund") to appeal.

Cancellation

Coin purchases are one-time payments and cannot be cancelled once completed; see refunds above. Paid calls are charged per started minute, and charging stops as soon as either person ends the call. If a call fails or drops because of a problem on our side, email us with the time of the call and we will review it and return coins where appropriate. You can stop using Bliz and delete your account at any time; unused coins are forfeited on deletion.

Joining and speaking in voice rooms is free. AI friends are free to chat with, up to 50 messages a day.

Host payouts

Hosts earn diamonds from calls and gifts at the rates shown in the app. Diamonds are held for 7 days before they can be withdrawn. 1 diamond = ₹0.20. The minimum withdrawal is 1,000 diamonds (₹200).

Before your first withdrawal you must complete payout verification: legal name, PAN, date of birth, a photo of your PAN card, a selfie, and a bank account or UPI ID in your own name. We review it ourselves, usually within 2 business days, and never ask you to pay for it. Approved withdrawals are paid by bank transfer or UPI through our bank, and we aim to process them within 7 business days.

Diamonds earned from free coins (welcome, daily, streak or referral coins) are zero: only coins people bought earn diamonds. If a payer's purchase is refunded, the diamonds it earned you are reversed; if you already withdrew them, further withdrawals are on hold until the amount is settled. Applicable taxes (such as TDS) may be deducted. A failed payout is returned to your diamond balance, and diamonds linked to fraud, refunds or rule violations may be withheld as described in our Terms.
