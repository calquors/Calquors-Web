Delete in the app

In the app: Me > ☰ (menu) > Settings > Delete account, then confirm. Your account is signed out and your profile is removed.

Delete without the app

Email support@calquors.com with the subject Bliz account deletion from your linked Google email, or include the mobile number registered on Bliz. We may contact you to confirm ownership before deleting. Never send passwords or login codes.

What is deleted and what is kept

We delete your profile, profile photo or avatar, host application details, selfie and voice introduction, messages, chats with AI friends, payout details (PAN, date of birth, bank or UPI details), follows, room memberships, linked Google account and push tokens, normally within 30 days. Unused coins and diamonds are forfeited. Coins you owe from a refunded purchase are not forgiven by deleting and re-joining: a new account on the same phone number does not get welcome coins again. We may keep limited records where needed for legal, tax, payment, fraud-prevention or safety reasons, such as purchase and payout transactions, moderation records and a marker that your phone number already received a welcome bonus. Tax records the law requires (name, PAN, amount paid and bank reference) are kept, encrypted, for 8 years. Copies of AI-friend chats already processed by our AI provider are kept by them only for the limited time their own terms allow. Messages other users have saved are outside our control.

Google Play purchases

Coin purchases are one-time and are not refunded on deletion except where Google Play policy or law requires. Deleting your account does not delete your Google Play order history.
