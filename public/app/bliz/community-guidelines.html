Be respectful

Bliz is for friendly conversation between adults. Treat people the way you want to be treated, in rooms, voice and video calls, chats and games.

Not allowed

  • Anyone under 18 using the app.
  • Harassment, bullying, hate speech, threats or abusive language targeted at a person or group.
  • Nudity or sexual content on video, sexual solicitation, or requests for photos or calls outside the app.
  • Hiding or covering your face on video calls, showing another person instead of yourself, or using fake or pre-recorded video.
  • Asking for money, UPI, bank details or gifts through pressure or deception; scams, fake giveaways, phishing links.
  • Impersonating another person, brand, host or Bliz staff.
  • Recording, screenshotting or sharing other users' voices, faces, names or personal information without consent.
  • Promoting violence, self-harm, drugs, weapons, gambling or any illegal activity.
  • Spam, advertising, multiple accounts, fake referrals or abuse of free calls and rewards.

Hosts

Hosts must use their own real selfie and voice, keep conversations friendly and within these guidelines, must not pressure users to spend coins, and must not move users to other apps or payment methods. Earnings from rule-breaking calls may be forfeited.

Report and block

Use the report and block options on any profile, room, call or chat. Our team reviews reports and may warn, mute, suspend or ban accounts. For urgent safety issues email support@calquors.com, and contact local emergency services (112 in India) if someone is in danger.
