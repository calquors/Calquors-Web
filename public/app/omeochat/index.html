About

OmeoChat brings people together through one-to-one video calls, text chats, group conversations and live voice rooms. Meet someone new, add friends, or join a group around shared interests.

Features

One-to-one video calls

Talk face to face with one person at a time.

Text and group chats

Message friends or chat with a whole group.

Live voice rooms

Join a room and talk with people who share your interests.

Friends list

Add the people you click with and find them again easily.

Optional map discovery

Find rooms on a map. Sharing a room location is always your choice.

Report and block tools

Report or block anyone, right from a chat or call.

Safety

OmeoChat is for adults aged 18 and older. Follow our Community Guidelines, respect consent, and use the report and block controls when needed. Never share sensitive personal information with strangers.

OmeoChat Plus

Optional Plus features are available through Google Play subscriptions. Prices are shown at checkout, and you can cancel anytime in Google Play.
