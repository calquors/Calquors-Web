Adults only; protect children

You must be at least 18. Do not create accounts for minors, involve minors in chats or contact minors through the service. Child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexual solicitation of minors and content facilitating child exploitation are prohibited.

Respect consent and safety

No nudity, sexual acts, sexually explicit content, threats, harassment, hate speech, stalking, graphic violence or encouragement of self-harm. Stop unwanted contact. Do not pressure anyone to appear on camera, share private details or continue a conversation.

Respect privacy

Do not reveal another person’s private information or capture or distribute their image, voice or messages without consent. Avoid sharing passwords, financial information or home addresses. A public room pin can reveal where a room was started.

Be authentic

Do not impersonate another person, company or service; claim a false affiliation; send spam; run scams; promote unlawful activity; or use bots to disrupt chats and rooms. Respect intellectual property rights.

Report, block and leave

Use in-app report and block controls, skip a conversation or leave a room when needed. Report suspected CSAE or CSAM using those controls or email hello@rivelolabs.com; do not send or redistribute illegal imagery. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services. We may remove content and restrict or terminate accounts for violations. Safety tools do not guarantee every conversation will be appropriate.
